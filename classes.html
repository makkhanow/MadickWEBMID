What to bring to your first class

Wear

A clean T-shirt and shorts without pockets. We lend you a gi for your first two classes.

Bring

A water bottle and a towel. Cut your fingernails and toenails before you come.

Know

Tell your coach about any injury. Take off rings and jewellery before you step on the mat.
