White belt: day one

What you learn in the first month

No fitness level needed. Every drill starts slow, with a partner who is learning too.

Escape

How to get out from under a heavier, stronger partner using timing and leverage instead of strength.

Control

How to hold a position so your partner cannot move, while you stay safe and breathe easily.

Finish

How joint locks and chokes work. You practise them slowly with a partner who taps the moment they feel pressure.

Five positions, one whole sport

Hover or tab to open a position.

Guard

You are on your back with your legs between you and your partner. It is your shield and your weapon.

Mount

One partner sits on the chest of the other. Hard to hold, harder to escape.

Side control

You pin your partner from the side, chest on chest, and look for the next move.

Back control

You are behind your partner with your legs hooked in. The strongest position in the sport.

Turtle

A defensive curl on hands and knees. Beginners learn to leave it before it becomes a trap.

Tap early. Tap often.

A tap on your partner or the mat means stop, and it is respected every time. It is how a club full of grapplers trains hard and goes home healthy.
